Frequently asked questions

Which public resolvers does this DNS checker query?

It queries Cloudflare DoH (1.1.1.1) and Google DoH (8.8.8.8) in parallel. Then it compares both answers for sync.

How does the ACME DNS-01 check work?

It queries TXT records at _acme-challenge.<hostname> on both resolvers and highlights active tokens.
