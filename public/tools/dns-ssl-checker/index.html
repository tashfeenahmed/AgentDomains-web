DNS Propagation & ACME DNS-01 Checker

Inspect live A, AAAA, CNAME, TXT, NS, and _acme-challenge records across Cloudflare (1.1.1.1) and Google (8.8.8.8) simultaneously using CORS-enabled DNS-over-HTTPS.

Enter any domain or subdomain and click Run Live DoH Check.
Need to publish an ACME DNS-01 challenge? With AgentDomains run agentdomains txt <label> <token> or invoke the add_acme_challenge MCP tool, wait 5 seconds, and re-run this debugger to confirm both 1.1.1.1 and 8.8.8.8 match.

Frequently asked questions

Which public resolvers does this DNS checker query?

It queries Cloudflare DoH (https://cloudflare-dns.com/dns-query) and Google Public DNS DoH (https://dns.google/resolve) directly from your browser in parallel and compares their answers for propagation consistency.

How does the ACME DNS-01 check work?

It automatically queries TXT records at _acme-challenge.<hostname> on both Cloudflare and Google resolvers and highlights whether an active ACME verification token is visible globally.
