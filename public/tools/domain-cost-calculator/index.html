Frequently asked questions

Why does this tool sometimes suggest Cloudflare Registrar or Porkbun?

AgentDomains provides managed subdomains, not transferable .com domains. If you need full TLD ownership, buy from a real registrar.

What pricing numbers power the live chart?

AgentDomains Pro is $10/yr (100 names). Cloudflare is $10.44/yr per .com, Porkbun $11.08/yr, Namecheap $15.88/yr renewal, ngrok $96/yr base, and x402 $59/yr + $25/domain.
