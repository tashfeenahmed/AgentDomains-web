Frequently asked questions

Why does the calculator sometimes recommend Porkbun or Cloudflare Registrar instead of AgentDomains?

Because AgentDomains is a managed subdomain service (*.makes.fyi and *.agentdomains.co), not a registrar. Whenever you check "Need transferable .com ownership", the calculator honestly recommends Cloudflare Registrar or Porkbun.

What pricing assumptions are used in the chart?

AgentDomains: $0 for 30-day trial (up to 10 names) or $10/yr Pro (up to 100 names). Cloudflare Registrar: ~$10.44/yr per .com. Porkbun: ~$11.08/yr per .com. Namecheap: ~$15.88/yr renewal per .com. ngrok: $96/yr base Personal seat + paid tier scaling. x402 Crypto Registrar (agentdomain.app reference): $59/yr Starter + ~$25/domain.
