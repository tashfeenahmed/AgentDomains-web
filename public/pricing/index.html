Free. Or $5.

Free covers everything we do: a hostname, DNS records, a forward, a reverse proxy with HTTPS, for up to 10 names. No card. Pro is for when you need more names, or a name that must not lapse.

This page is the whole pricing model · as of 2026-09-27

Names already registered keep working. We said this page would change first if pricing ever changed, and here it is, updated before Pro went on sale. Adding Pro took nothing away from free: the same 10 names, every record type, forwards, proxies and delegation, and still no card. Pro only adds things.

Two plans

Free

$0 no card
  • Up to 10 names per account, under makes.fyi or agentdomains.co
  • A, AAAA, CNAME and TXT records, plus nameserver delegation
  • URL forwarding
  • Reverse proxy with HTTPS on our certificate
  • A name whose origin stops answering gets an email at 30 days and may be released at 45, so abandoned names go back to the pool
  • Email support, best effort

Pro

$5 / month, or $48 / year
  • Up to 100 names per account
  • Everything in Free, unchanged
  • Never released for being unreachable. You still get the email if a name goes dark, but the name stays yours
  • Priority email support: Pro requests are answered first
  • Cancel any time from the billing portal. You keep every name you hold; past 10 you just can't add more
# from the CLI: prints a Stripe checkout link, opens it on a terminal
$ agentdomains upgrade
$ agentdomains upgrade --yearly
# card, invoices, switch to yearly, cancel
$ agentdomains billing

Over MCP it is the upgrade_to_pro tool; over HTTP, POST /v1/billing/checkout. Either way you get a link and a person pays. Agents can't charge you. Payments are handled by Stripe, and we never see your card.

What every plan gets

A real hostname

A name under makes.fyi or agentdomains.co, resolving on the public internet in seconds. 10 names per account on Free, 100 on Pro.

DNS records

A, AAAA, CNAME and TXT, added and removed one at a time, plus nameserver delegation if you would rather run the zone yourself. No per-record or per-query charge.

Forwarding

Point the name at any URL and the edge redirects it. No metering, no bandwidth bill.

A reverse proxy with HTTPS

Serve your backend at the name with TLS terminated at our edge, on our certificate. Nothing to install on your origin, and nothing to pay for the certificate.

No request quota, no rate card, no overage. The signup endpoint is rate-limited per IP to keep it from being farmed, and that is the extent of it.

What we don't do

Register real domains

We are not a registrar. We cannot sell you example.com, or any second-level domain under a public TLD, at any price.

Transfers

A name here is a subdomain we operate. There is no auth code, no transfer out, and no way to take it to another provider. It exists as long as we keep running.

WHOIS / RDAP

Subdomains have no registration record to publish, so there is nothing to look up, nothing to privacy-protect, and no registrant contact to keep current.

Read this before you build on it. Free and instant is the right trade for scaffolding an agent just produced. It is the wrong trade for a name that has to matter in two years, carry a brand, or hold an email address.
