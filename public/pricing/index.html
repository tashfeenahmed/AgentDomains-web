30 days free. Then $1.

Every new account gets a 30-day free trial with everything we do: a hostname, DNS records, a forward, a reverse proxy with HTTPS, for up to 10 names. No card. To keep your names after day 30, go Pro for $10/year or $1/month.

This page is the whole pricing model · as of 2026-11-13

How the trial works. Day 1: sign up and claim, no card. Agents can do it on their own. Day 25: we email a reminder with the end date. Day 30: names on accounts that have not upgraded are released, so anyone can claim them. Your account and API key stay, and upgrading lets you claim again.

Two plans

Free trial

$0 for 30 days, no card
  • Up to 10 names per account, under agentdomains.co or makes.fyi
  • A, AAAA, CNAME and TXT records, plus nameserver delegation
  • URL forwarding
  • Reverse proxy with HTTPS on our certificate
  • A reminder email on day 25, with the end date

  • Email support, best effort

Pro

  • Names kept as long as you pay
  • 100 names instead of 10
  • Visitor stats for names you forward or proxy: daily visits per name
  • Never removed for being unused, even when the server behind them goes quiet
  • Priority support: Pro requests are answered first
  • Everything in the trial
  • Cancel any time from the billing portal

Upgrade with your API key

Paste the key your agent (or the CLI) uses, pick a plan, and we send you to Stripe to pay.

Your key never leaves this browser except to api.agentdomains.co, and it is not stored. Already on Pro? The same button opens your billing portal. Lost the key? Get a new one. Want to see your names first? Manage them.

# from the CLI: prints a Stripe checkout link, opens it on a terminal
$ agentdomains upgrade --yearly
$ agentdomains upgrade
# card, invoices, switch to yearly, cancel
$ agentdomains billing

Over MCP it is the upgrade_to_pro tool; over HTTP, POST /v1/billing/checkout. Either way you get a link and a person pays. Agents can't charge you. Payments are handled by Stripe, and we never see your card.

What every plan gets

A real hostname

A name under agentdomains.co or makes.fyi, resolving on the public internet in seconds. 10 names per account on the trial, 100 on Pro.

DNS records

A, AAAA, CNAME and TXT, added and removed one at a time, plus nameserver delegation if you would rather run the zone yourself. No per-record or per-query charge.

Forwarding

Point the name at any URL and the edge redirects it. No metering, no bandwidth bill.

A reverse proxy with HTTPS

Serve your backend at the name with TLS terminated at our edge, on our certificate. Nothing to install on your origin, and nothing to pay for the certificate.

No request quota, no rate card, no overage. The signup endpoint is rate-limited per IP to keep it from being farmed, and that is the extent of it.

What we don't do

Register real domains

We are not a registrar. We cannot sell you example.com, or any second-level domain under a public TLD, at any price.

Transfers

A name here is a subdomain we operate. There is no auth code, no transfer out, and no way to take it to another provider. It exists as long as we keep running.

WHOIS / RDAP

Subdomains have no registration record to publish, so there is nothing to look up, nothing to privacy-protect, and no registrant contact to keep current.

Read this before you build on it. Cheap and instant is the right trade for scaffolding an agent just produced. It is the wrong trade for a name that has to matter in two years, carry a brand, or hold an email address. For those, buy a domain you own. Pricing questions

What happens on day 30?

If the account has not upgraded, its names are released: deleted, and free for anyone to claim. The account and API key stay. Upgrade any time and you can claim names again.

Do I need a card to start?

No. Signup and claims need no card, so an agent can do both on its own. A person pays only if you pick Pro.

I had an account before the trial

Accounts made before this change got a fresh 30 days from 13 November 2026, so their trial ends on 13 December 2026. We emailed each of them about it.
