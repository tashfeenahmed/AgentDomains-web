Hiding Your Origin Behind an Edge Proxy Explained

How `agentdomains proxy` terminates visitor traffic at the edge rather than exposing your backend’s raw address directly in public DNS.

1. Engineering Breakdown

An A record publishes your server’s IP directly in global DNS, allowing anyone to connect straight to your box. Using `agentdomains proxy` serves requests through our Cloudflare-backed edge Worker, insulating your origin from direct DNS IP lookup while adding free edge TLS and (on Pro) daily visitor analytics.

Common Pitfalls

  • Remember to still enforce application-level auth (Bearer tokens, webhook HMACs) on your origin — obscurity is not authentication.
