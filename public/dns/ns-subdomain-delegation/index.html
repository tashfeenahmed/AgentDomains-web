NS Subdomain Delegation Explained

Hand authoritative control of your entire subdomain tree (`*.myapp.makes.fyi`) to your own nameservers (Cloudflare, Route 53, deSEC, or BIND) for MX, SRV, and custom DNSSEC.

1. Engineering Breakdown

NS delegation tells recursive resolvers to ask your chosen authoritative nameservers for `myapp.makes.fyi` and every label beneath it. Once delegated, you manage MX, SRV, CAA, and arbitrary deep subtrees directly on your own DNS provider.

Common Pitfalls

  • Supply at least two valid authoritative nameservers that already have a configured zone for `myapp.makes.fyi`.
  • Delegating NS removes AgentDomains edge forwards/proxies on that label because your nameservers become authoritative.

Frequently asked questions

  

    
What is NS Subdomain Delegation?
Hand authoritative control of your entire subdomain tree (`*.myapp.makes.fyi`) to your own nameservers (Cloudflare, Route 53, deSEC, or BIND) for MX, SRV, and custom DNSSEC.

    
How do I automate NS Subdomain Delegation with AgentDomains?
Run `agentdomains ns myapp ns1.desec.io ns2.desec.org` from the Go CLI, call the REST API over curl, or invoke the `delegate_nameservers` tool on https://mcp.agentdomains.co.
