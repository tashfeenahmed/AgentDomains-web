Frequently asked questions

Which DNS record types can I manage on AgentDomains?

AgentDomains supports A, AAAA, CNAME, and TXT records (including _acme-challenge and deeper sub-labels via --host), plus NS delegation for full zone control, edge HTTPS reverse proxying, and 301/302 URL forwarding.

How do I debug live DNS propagation or ACME DNS-01 tokens?

Use our free browser-based /tools/dns-ssl-checker which queries Cloudflare (1.1.1.1) and Google (8.8.8.8) DoH resolvers simultaneously for A, AAAA, CNAME, TXT, NS, and _acme-challenge records.
