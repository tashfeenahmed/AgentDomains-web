Preventing Dangling Subdomain Takeovers Explained

Always remove DNS records, proxies, or the subdomain itself *before* deleting your underlying Fly.io, Railway, Heroku, or S3 origin.

1. Engineering Breakdown

A dangling DNS takeover happens when `myapp.makes.fyi` still CNAMEs or proxies to `old-app.fly.dev` after you deleted `old-app` on Fly.io — allowing someone else to claim `old-app.fly.dev` and serve arbitrary content under your subdomain. Teardown order matters: delete DNS/proxy first, destroy cloud compute second.

Common Pitfalls

  • In CI PR teardown jobs, put `agentdomains delete` BEFORE `fly apps destroy` so no dangling window ever exists.
