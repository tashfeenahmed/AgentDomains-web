nslookup vs dig vs DoH Explained

Why `dig` gives cleaner TTL/authority output than `nslookup`, and when browser DoH tools beat both.

1. Engineering Breakdown

`nslookup` ships by default on Windows and macOS, making it handy in a pinch (`nslookup -type=TXT _acme-challenge.myapp.makes.fyi 1.1.1.1`), but `dig` shows exact TTLs, flags (`aa`, `ad`), and query latency cleanly. When working from a restricted corporate network or browser, our live DoH `/tools/dns-ssl-checker` queries both `1.1.1.1` and `8.8.8.8` simultaneously.

Common Pitfalls

  • Both `nslookup` and `dig` default to your OS resolver unless you explicitly append `@1.1.1.1` or `8.8.8.8`.
