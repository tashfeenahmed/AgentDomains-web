HSTS and Subdomain Security Explained

Understand how `Strict-Transport-Security` enforces HTTPS on your subdomain and why you should avoid `includeSubDomains` until every sub-label has valid TLS.

1. Engineering Breakdown

HSTS tells browsers to automatically upgrade all `http://` requests to `https://` for `max-age` seconds, preventing SSL-stripping attacks. Start with a short `max-age=300` while testing your Caddy/Nginx TLS config before extending to `31536000`.

Common Pitfalls

  • Never enable HSTS if you intend to test plain HTTP on a sub-label later — browsers refuse to downgrade until `max-age` expires.

HTTP-01 vs DNS-01 SSL Validation

Wildcard SSL on Subdomains

          
Issue a single `*.myapp.makes.fyi` TLS certificate covering unlimited deeper sub-labels (`a.myapp.makes.fyi`, `b.myapp.makes.fyi`) via ACME DNS-01.

Let’s Encrypt Rate Limits on Subdomains
