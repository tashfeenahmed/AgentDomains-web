Certbot Manual & Hooked DNS-01 Explained

Automate Certbot `--manual-auth-hook` and `--manual-cleanup-hook` with `agentdomains txt` and `agentdomains unrecord` for unattended renewals.

1. Engineering Breakdown

Certbot passes `$CERTBOT_VALIDATION` to your `--manual-auth-hook` script. A 3-line shell hook calling `agentdomains txt myapp "$CERTBOT_VALIDATION"` and sleeping 10 seconds lets Certbot renew certificates cleanly even with port 80 completely firewalled.

Common Pitfalls

  • Save the returned `id` from `add_acme_challenge` so your `--manual-cleanup-hook` can call `agentdomains unrecord myapp <id>`.

Frequently asked questions

  

    
What is Certbot DNS-01 Hook?
Automate Certbot `--manual-auth-hook` and `--manual-cleanup-hook` with `agentdomains txt` and `agentdomains unrecord` for unattended renewals.

    
How do I automate Certbot Manual & Hooked DNS-01 with AgentDomains?
Run `agentdomains txt myapp "$CERTBOT_VALIDATION"` from the Go CLI, call the REST API over curl, or invoke the `add_acme_challenge` tool on https://mcp.agentdomains.co.
