Zone Apex vs Subdomain DNS Explained

Why a subdomain like `myapp.makes.fyi` can cleanly hold a standard CNAME record while root apex domains (`example.com`) require CNAME flattening or ALIAS tricks.

1. Engineering Breakdown

RFC 1034 prohibits placing a CNAME record at the root of a DNS zone because the apex already owns SOA and NS records. Because `myapp.makes.fyi` is a subdomain inside the `makes.fyi` zone, you can point it straight at Fly.io, Railway, or Render with a standard RFC-compliant CNAME record.

Common Pitfalls

  • Remember you can also add deeper sub-labels (`--host www` or `--host api`) under your claimed subdomain.
