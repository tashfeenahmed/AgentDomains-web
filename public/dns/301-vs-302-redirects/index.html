301 vs 302 HTTP Redirects Explained

302 is temporary and safe to change anytime (AgentDomains default); 301 is permanent and aggressively cached by browsers and search engines.

1. Engineering Breakdown

HTTP 302 Found tells browsers and crawlers to check your AgentDomains edge again on future visits, making it ideal while iterating on demo destinations. HTTP 301 Moved Permanently instructs browsers to cache the destination indefinitely and signals search engines to transfer ranking signals to the target URL.

Common Pitfalls

  • Avoid setting `permanent: true` (301) during active testing — browsers will cling to the old target even after you update the forward.
  • Keep `preserve_path: true` (the default) so `/docs?v=2` cleanly forwards to `https://example.com/docs?v=2`.

Frequently asked questions

  

    
What is 301 vs 302 Redirect?
302 is temporary and safe to change anytime (AgentDomains default); 301 is permanent and aggressively cached by browsers and search engines.

    
How do I automate 301 vs 302 HTTP Redirects with AgentDomains?
Run `agentdomains forward myapp https://example.com --permanent` from the Go CLI, call the REST API over curl, or invoke the `set_forward` tool on https://mcp.agentdomains.co.
