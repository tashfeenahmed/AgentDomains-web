Origin-to-edge flow for Twilio Voice & SMS Webhooks: connect voice-agent.fly.dev to twilio-voice.agentdomains.co with automatic HTTPS.

1. Choose Mode: Edge Proxy vs DNS-01

Edge HTTPS Reverse Proxy

RECOMMENDED
  • Point at any public hostname without https://.
  • Cloudflare edge handles TLS 1.3 automatically.
  • Zero Certbot config and zero IP exposure.

A Record + ACME DNS-01

FOR BARE VPS
  • Point directly at your server IPv4 or IPv6 address.
  • Issue certs via Caddy or agentdomains txt DNS-01.
  • Works even when your firewall blocks port 80.
Twilio Voice & SMS Webhooks — 3-command AgentDomains setup

Frequently asked questions

  

    
Should I use edge proxy or an A record for Twilio Voice & SMS Webhooks?
Use agentdomains proxy for TwiML webhooks. Use an A record and Caddy for high-volume Media Streams WebSockets.

    
How do I check DNS propagation for Twilio Voice & SMS Webhooks?
Open /tools/dns-ssl-checker or run dig @1.1.1.1 twilio-voice.agentdomains.co. Confirm both Cloudflare and Google return your record.

    
Can an AI agent configure Twilio Voice & SMS Webhooks routing via MCP?
Yes. Connect your client to https://mcp.agentdomains.co. Ask it to call claim_domain and set_proxy.
