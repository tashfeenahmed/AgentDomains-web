3. Honest Verdict: Which Should You Pick?

Pick Tailscale Funnel if…

ALTERNATIVE
  • Your service runs inside a Tailscale mesh and needs NAT traversal with no open router ports.
  • You do not mind sharing an ugly tailnet suffix like box.tail9a2f1.ts.net.

Pick AgentDomains if…

AGENT-FIRST
  • You want a clean hostname like bot.makes.fyi. You can even proxy straight to your Tailscale Funnel host.
  • You need custom DNS records (A, AAAA, CNAME, TXT) or webhook domains unrelated to a Tailscale daemon.

Frequently asked questions

Does AgentDomains replace Tailscale Funnel for buying a .com domain?

No. AgentDomains provides managed subdomains under makes.fyi and agentdomains.co. Buy a transferable .com at Cloudflare Registrar or Porkbun.

How does pricing compare between AgentDomains and Tailscale Funnel?

AgentDomains offers a 30-day free trial (10 names, no card) and $10/yr Pro (100 names). Tailscale Funnel: $0 Personal (tied to tailnet machine names, max 3 users).

Can an AI agent switch from Tailscale Funnel to AgentDomains via MCP?

Yes. Connect to https://mcp.agentdomains.co and call signup, claim_domain, and set_proxy or add_dns_record.
