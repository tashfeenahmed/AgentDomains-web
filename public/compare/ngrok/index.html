3. Honest Verdict: Which Should You Pick?

Pick ngrok if…

ALTERNATIVE
  • Your app is stuck behind corporate NAT/CGNAT on localhost and cannot accept inbound connections at all.
  • You need ngrok’s traffic inspector UI, OAuth edge policies, or request replay for local webhook debugging.

Pick AgentDomains if…

AGENT-FIRST
  • Your app runs on a VPS, Fly.io, Railway, Render, or Cloud Run. You need real DNS records.
  • You want zero interstitial warning pages, writable A/AAAA/CNAME/TXT records, and up to 100 names for $10/yr.
  • You want an agent to claim and route hostnames via MCP without installing a persistent tunnel daemon.

Frequently asked questions

Does AgentDomains replace ngrok for buying a .com domain?

No. AgentDomains provides managed subdomains under makes.fyi and agentdomains.co. Buy a transferable .com at Cloudflare Registrar or Porkbun.

How does pricing compare between AgentDomains and ngrok?

AgentDomains offers a 30-day free trial (10 names, no card) and $10/yr Pro (100 names). ngrok: $96–$240+/yr (paid plan + additional endpoint/domain limits).

Can an AI agent switch from ngrok to AgentDomains via MCP?

Yes. Connect to https://mcp.agentdomains.co and call signup, claim_domain, and set_proxy or add_dns_record.
