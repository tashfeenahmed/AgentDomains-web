3. Honest Verdict: Which Should You Choose?

Choose ngrok if…

  • Your app is stuck behind corporate NAT/CGNAT on localhost and cannot accept inbound connections at all.
  • You need ngrok’s traffic inspector UI, OAuth edge policies, or request replay for local webhook debugging.

Choose AgentDomains if…

  • Your app already runs on a public VPS, Fly.io, Railway, Render, or Cloud Run and needs a clean hostname with real DNS records.
  • You want zero interstitial warning pages, writable A/AAAA/CNAME/TXT records, and up to 100 names for $10/yr.
  • You want an agent to claim and route hostnames via MCP without installing a persistent tunnel daemon.

Frequently asked questions

Does AgentDomains replace ngrok for buying a .com domain?

No. AgentDomains provides managed subdomains under agentdomains.co and makes.fyi — not transferable ICANN second-level domains. If you must legally own and transfer a .com brand name, use a registrar like Cloudflare Registrar or Porkbun.

How does pricing compare between AgentDomains and ngrok?

AgentDomains starts with a 30-day free trial (10 names, no card) and Pro is $10/yr ($1/mo) for up to 100 names with zero bandwidth bills. ngrok: Free tier (1 static domain, interstitial banner on web); Personal $8/mo ($96/yr); Pro $20/mo.

Can an AI agent switch from ngrok to AgentDomains via MCP?

Yes. Connect your agent to https://mcp.agentdomains.co (or npx -y agentdomains-mcp) and use signup, claim_domain, and set_proxy or add_dns_record.
