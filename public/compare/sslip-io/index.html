3. Honest Verdict: Which Should You Pick?

Pick sslip.io if…

ALTERNATIVE
  • You need an instant IP-mapped hostname in a CI cluster without making any API call at all.

Pick AgentDomains if…

AGENT-FIRST
  • You want clean demo URLs without raw IP addresses, plus automatic edge HTTPS without configuring Certbot.
  • You need CNAME records for Fly.io/Railway/Render or TXT records for webhook and OAuth verification.
  • You want to swap backend IPs or origins without changing the URL handed to clients or webhooks.

Frequently asked questions

Does AgentDomains replace sslip.io for buying a .com domain?

No. AgentDomains provides managed subdomains under makes.fyi and agentdomains.co. Buy a transferable .com at Cloudflare Registrar or Porkbun.

How does pricing compare between AgentDomains and sslip.io?

AgentDomains offers a 30-day free trial (10 names, no card) and $10/yr Pro (100 names). sslip.io: $0 (IP-encoded hostnames only).

Can an AI agent switch from sslip.io to AgentDomains via MCP?

Yes. Connect to https://mcp.agentdomains.co and call signup, claim_domain, and set_proxy or add_dns_record.
