3. Honest Verdict: Which Should You Pick?

Pick Porkbun + MCP if…

ALTERNATIVE
  • You need a transferable .com, .dev, or .io domain with full WHOIS ownership and custom email.
  • The project is a permanent brand or production SaaS — AgentDomains explicitly does NOT sell transferable TLDs.

Pick AgentDomains if…

AGENT-FIRST
  • Your agent builds 10 to 100 demos or webhooks. Buying a .com for each costs too much.
  • You want an instant card-free 30-day trial, native 20-tool hosted MCP server, and built-in edge HTTPS reverse proxy.

Frequently asked questions

Does AgentDomains replace Porkbun + MCP for buying a .com domain?

No. AgentDomains provides managed subdomains under makes.fyi and agentdomains.co. Buy a transferable .com at Cloudflare Registrar or Porkbun.

How does pricing compare between AgentDomains and Porkbun + MCP?

AgentDomains offers a 30-day free trial (10 names, no card) and $10/yr Pro (100 names). Porkbun + MCP: ~$110.80/yr for 10 .com domains.

Can an AI agent switch from Porkbun + MCP to AgentDomains via MCP?

Yes. Connect to https://mcp.agentdomains.co and call signup, claim_domain, and set_proxy or add_dns_record.
