3. Honest Verdict: Which Should You Choose?

Choose Spaceship if…

  • You want to purchase and own transferable .com or budget TLDs at competitive renewal rates.

Choose AgentDomains if…

  • You want card-free agent provisioning, a hosted MCP endpoint (`https://mcp.agentdomains.co`), and built-in HTTPS proxying.

Frequently asked questions

Does AgentDomains replace Spaceship for buying a .com domain?

No. AgentDomains provides managed subdomains under agentdomains.co and makes.fyi — not transferable ICANN second-level domains. If you must legally own and transfer a .com brand name, use a registrar like Cloudflare Registrar or Porkbun.

How does pricing compare between AgentDomains and Spaceship?

AgentDomains starts with a 30-day free trial (10 names, no card) and Pro is $10/yr ($1/mo) for up to 100 names with zero bandwidth bills. Spaceship: Low-cost TLD registrations (~$9.98/yr per .com renewal); paid per domain.

Can an AI agent switch from Spaceship to AgentDomains via MCP?

Yes. Connect your agent to https://mcp.agentdomains.co (or npx -y agentdomains-mcp) and use signup, claim_domain, and set_proxy or add_dns_record.
