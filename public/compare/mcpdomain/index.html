Frequently asked questions

Does AgentDomains replace MCPDomain for buying a .com domain?

No. AgentDomains provides managed subdomains under makes.fyi and agentdomains.co. Buy a transferable .com at Cloudflare Registrar or Porkbun.

How does pricing compare between AgentDomains and MCPDomain?

AgentDomains offers a 30-day free trial (10 names, no card) and $10/yr Pro (100 names). MCPDomain: ~$140+/yr for 10 domains.

Can an AI agent switch from MCPDomain to AgentDomains via MCP?

Yes. Connect to https://mcp.agentdomains.co and call signup, claim_domain, and set_proxy or add_dns_record.
