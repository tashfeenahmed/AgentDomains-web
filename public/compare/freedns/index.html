3. Honest Verdict: Which Should You Choose?

Choose FreeDNS (afraid.org) if…

  • You want a quirky community-donated domain suffix and only need manual DNS records for a hobby box.

Choose AgentDomains if…

  • You do not want your subdomain controlled by a random third-party domain donor who can pull the domain offline.
  • You want a clean REST API, Go CLI, 20-tool MCP server, and built-in edge HTTPS reverse proxy.

Frequently asked questions

Does AgentDomains replace FreeDNS (afraid.org) for buying a .com domain?

No. AgentDomains provides managed subdomains under agentdomains.co and makes.fyi — not transferable ICANN second-level domains. If you must legally own and transfer a .com brand name, use a registrar like Cloudflare Registrar or Porkbun.

How does pricing compare between AgentDomains and FreeDNS (afraid.org)?

AgentDomains starts with a 30-day free trial (10 names, no card) and Pro is $10/yr ($1/mo) for up to 100 names with zero bandwidth bills. FreeDNS (afraid.org): Free (up to 5 subdomains on community-contributed domains); Premium $5/mo ($60/yr).

Can an AI agent switch from FreeDNS (afraid.org) to AgentDomains via MCP?

Yes. Connect your agent to https://mcp.agentdomains.co (or npx -y agentdomains-mcp) and use signup, claim_domain, and set_proxy or add_dns_record.
