AgentDomains vs DuckDNS

An honest, citation-backed breakdown of AgentDomains vs DuckDNS (duckdns.org) for AI agent deployments, DNS automation, edge HTTPS proxying, and 1-year total cost.

3. Honest Verdict: Which Should You Choose?

Choose DuckDNS if…

  • You only need 1–5 free forever dynamic DNS names pointed at a home IPv4/IPv6 box.
  • You do not need CNAME records, edge HTTPS reverse proxying, or an MCP server.
  • You are comfortable signing in with a human OAuth account and running your own reverse proxy/TLS.

Choose AgentDomains if…

  • Your AI agent needs to claim names unattended via CLI or 20-tool MCP server.
  • You need CNAME records, NS delegation, URL forwarding, or zero-config edge HTTPS reverse proxying in front of PaaS hosts.
  • You want 10 names on a card-free 30-day trial and up to 100 names for $10/yr.

Frequently asked questions

Does AgentDomains replace DuckDNS for buying a .com domain?

No. AgentDomains provides managed subdomains under agentdomains.co and makes.fyi — not transferable ICANN second-level domains. If you must legally own and transfer a .com brand name, use a registrar like Cloudflare Registrar or Porkbun.

How does pricing compare between AgentDomains and DuckDNS?

AgentDomains starts with a 30-day free trial (10 names, no card) and Pro is $10/yr ($1/mo) for up to 100 names with zero bandwidth bills. DuckDNS: Free (up to 5 subdomains per account; donation-supported).

Can an AI agent switch from DuckDNS to AgentDomains via MCP?

Yes. Connect your agent to https://mcp.agentdomains.co (or npx -y agentdomains-mcp) and use signup, claim_domain, and set_proxy or add_dns_record.
