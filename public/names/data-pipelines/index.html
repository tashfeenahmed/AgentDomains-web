How to name & route Data Pipelines & ETL

Recommended Pattern

DEV & INFRA
  • Use predictable Data Pipelines & ETL names like etl-hub.agentdomains.co in CI pipelines, webhook dashboards, and OAuth allowlists.
  • Point an A record at a bare VPS IPv4 address, or use agentdomains proxy when your service runs on Railway, Render, or Fly.io.
  • Publish _acme-challenge TXT tokens with agentdomains txt when your firewall blocks port 80.
