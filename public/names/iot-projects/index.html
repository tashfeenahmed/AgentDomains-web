Frequently asked questions

How do I claim one of these IoT & Hardware Projects subdomains?

Click "Copy claim" on any available card and paste the command into your terminal (after running agentdomains signup), or ask your MCP-connected agent to call claim_domain for that label.

Can I point a IoT & Hardware Projects subdomain at my own VPS, Fly.io, or Railway app?

Yes. Every claimed makes.fyi or agentdomains.co subdomain supports A, AAAA, CNAME, TXT, NS delegation, URL forwarding, and automatic HTTPS reverse proxying.
