Frequently asked questions

How do I claim one of these Design Tools subdomains?

Click COPY CLAIM on any free card and run it in your terminal. Or ask your MCP agent to call claim_domain.

Can I point a Design Tools subdomain at my VPS, Fly.io, or Railway app?

Yes. Every subdomain supports A, AAAA, CNAME, TXT, NS delegation, URL forwarding, and edge HTTPS proxying.

What happens after the 30-day free trial?

We email a reminder on day 25. Upgrade to Pro ($10/yr or $1/mo) to keep up to 100 names permanently.
